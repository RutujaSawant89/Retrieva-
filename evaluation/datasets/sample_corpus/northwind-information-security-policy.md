# Northwind Analytics — Information Security Policy

Version 3.2 · Owner: Chief Information Security Officer · Fictional policy for demonstrating Retrieva.

## 1 Purpose and Scope

This policy sets the minimum security requirements for all Northwind employees, contractors and systems that store or process company or customer information.

## 2 Access Control

### 2.1 Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all corporate systems, including email, source code repositories and production infrastructure. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems.

### 2.2 Access reviews

Managers review the access rights of their team members every quarter. Access to production systems is reviewed monthly by the security team. Accounts of departing staff are disabled within 24 hours of their last working day.

### 2.3 Least privilege

Employees receive only the access required for their role. Administrative privileges are granted for a maximum of 12 months and must be re-approved.

## 3 Data Classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer Data is always classified as Restricted. Restricted information must be encrypted at rest and in transit and must never be stored on personal devices.

## 4 Incident Response

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery through the incident hotline. The security team classifies incidents by severity; Severity 1 incidents trigger the incident response plan and an executive briefing within four hours. Personal data breaches are notified to affected customers within 48 hours, in line with customer contracts.

## 5 Vulnerability Management

Critical vulnerabilities in internet-facing systems must be patched within 7 days of a fix becoming available; high-severity vulnerabilities within 30 days. External penetration tests are performed at least once a year by an independent firm.

## 6 Backups

Production databases are backed up every 6 hours. Backups are encrypted, stored in a separate region, and retained for 35 days. Restore procedures are tested quarterly.

## 7 Security Awareness

All staff complete security awareness training when they join and annually thereafter. Phishing simulation exercises are run every quarter.
