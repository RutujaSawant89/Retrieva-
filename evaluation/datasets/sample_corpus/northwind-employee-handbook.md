# Northwind Analytics — Employee Handbook

Fictional handbook for demonstrating Retrieva. Applies to all full-time employees.

## Working Hours

The standard working week is 37.5 hours. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with their manager's approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the following year and must be used by 31 March.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive full pay for up to 10 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive working days.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of paid parental leave, which may be taken within the first year after birth or adoption.

## Equipment

Company laptops are refreshed every three years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

## Learning and Development

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses, conferences and certifications, subject to manager approval.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days with itemised receipts. Travel must be booked through the company travel portal; economy class is standard for flights under six hours.
