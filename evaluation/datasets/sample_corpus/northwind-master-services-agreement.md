# Northwind Analytics — Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Northwind Analytics Ltd. ("Provider") and the customer identified in the Order Form ("Customer"). This is a fictional agreement created for demonstrating Retrieva.

## 1 Definitions

"Services" means the hosted data analytics platform and related support described in Schedule A. "Customer Data" means all data submitted to the Services by or on behalf of the Customer. "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by the Provider to process Customer Data.

## 2 Term

The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date. Thereafter the Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.

## 3 Fees and Payment

### 3.1 Subscription fees

The Customer shall pay an annual subscription fee of 120,000 USD, invoiced annually in advance. Fees for additional seats are 450 USD per seat per year, prorated for partial years.

### 3.2 Payment terms

Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5 percent per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

### 3.3 Price changes

The Provider may increase fees at renewal by no more than five (5) percent per renewal term, with at least ninety (90) days written notice.

## 4 Service Levels

The Provider shall make the Services available 99.9 percent of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance. If availability falls below 99.9 percent, the Customer receives a service credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee; below 99.0 percent the credit is 25 percent. Service credits are the Customer's sole remedy for availability failures.

## 5 Data Protection and Security

### 5.1 Encryption

The Provider shall encrypt Customer Data at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.

### 5.2 Location

Customer Data is stored and processed only in data centres located in the European Union, unless the Customer gives prior written consent.

### 5.3 Breach notification

The Provider shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach affecting Customer Data without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it.

### 5.4 Subprocessors

The Provider shall give the Customer at least thirty (30) days notice before engaging a new Subprocessor. The Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds.

## 6 Termination

### 6.1 Termination for convenience

The Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days prior written notice. Prepaid fees for the remainder of the term are not refundable on termination for convenience.

### 6.2 Termination for cause

Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.

### 6.3 Data return

Within thirty (30) days after termination the Provider shall make Customer Data available for export, and shall delete it within ninety (90) days after termination unless retention is required by law.

## 7 Limitation of Liability

Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality, data protection obligations, or a party's indemnification obligations.

## 8 Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ireland. The courts of Dublin have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
